# Dataset Documentation

## Purpose

This dataset is used to develop and evaluate the Provenance
AI Detector for college admissions-style essays.

The detector is designed to distinguish between writing patterns
rather than determine authorship with certainty.

## Dataset Categories

### Human

Essays written by humans without AI assistance.

### AI

Essays generated by language models.

### Hybrid

Human-written essays that were subsequently modified,
polished, or rewritten using AI assistance.

## Planned Coverage

The dataset should contain essays covering multiple subjects
and writing styles.

Examples include:

- Personal experiences
- Academic interests
- Career goals
- Community involvement
- Challenges and achievements
- Creative/personal prompts

## Important Limitations

The dataset must not be treated as representative of all
human writing or all AI-generated writing.

Performance may vary depending on:

- essay topic
- author background
- writing proficiency
- English language proficiency
- model used to generate text
- amount of AI assistance
- essay length

## Evaluation

The final detector will be evaluated on held-out essays
that are not used during feature development or training.

Evaluation will include:

- overall accuracy
- precision
- recall
- F1 score
- confusion matrix
- confidently incorrect examples
- analysis of false positives
- analysis of false negatives

Particular attention will be given to whether writers
who learned English as a second language are disproportionately
flagged.
