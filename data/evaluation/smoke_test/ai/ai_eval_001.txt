Artificial intelligence is transforming modern education by providing students with personalized and accessible learning experiences. AI-powered platforms can analyze student performance, identify areas of difficulty, and recommend appropriate learning resources. Furthermore, intelligent tutoring systems can provide immediate feedback and allow students to learn at their own pace. However, the integration of AI into education also raises concerns regarding privacy, academic integrity, and excessive dependence on automated systems. Therefore, educational institutions should adopt artificial intelligence responsibly while maintaining meaningful human involvement in the learning process.
