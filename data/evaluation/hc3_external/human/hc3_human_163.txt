The chin numbness could be related to a pinched nerve, which is a common condition in diabetic persons too. So, there is no direct connection between the two conditions, but diabetes, may lead to this complex clinical situation. Anyway, I would first recommend performing a stool analysis to exclude any possible GI infection. It is also necessary to closely monitor her blood electrolytes to investigate for any electrolyte imbalance caused by diarrhea.
