A puddle dries by evaporation , which is when the water molecules get enough energy to turn into water vapour . Now , on a normal day you would think that the water is n't warm enough to boil and evaporate , which is true . However water does n't need to boil to evaporate . As the water molecules move around and bump into each other , they are constantly giving or losing energy to each other . So when a molecule is lucky enough to just keep getting more energy , it will eventually have enough energy to break free from the puddle and turn into water vapour ( steam is just hot water vapour ) . Eventually , all the water molecules will have gotten enough just energy from bumping into each to evaporate .
