you and someone else you re related to , go up the family tree to find your most recent common ancestor . if you share a grandparent , you are cousins , if you share a great - grandparent , you are second cousins , if your grandparent is the others great - grandparent , you are 1st cousins once removed basically take you and the other person , find the common ancestor . find the number of " greats " between you and the ancestor ( plus 1 , the word grandparent counts as 1 great ) , now find it for the other person . which ever number is lower is what " number " cousins you are ( 1st , 2nd , 3rd , etc ) the difference between the two is the degree of separation example : my great - great - great - great - grandma ( so that would be a 5 ... 4 greats + 1 grandma ) is your great - great - great - great - great - great - great - great - grandma ( that s a 9 ) . the lower number is 5 so we are 5th cousins , and the difference between the two is 4 , so we are 5th cousins 4 times removed
