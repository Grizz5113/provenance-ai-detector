A computer ca n't really run hundreds upon hundreds of tasks at once unless it 's got hundreds of processors running in parallel . The operating system of a computer schedules processes to run on the processors given priority of each task . Because of the speed of computation , this can make it seem like many processes are all running at once , but at any given fraction of a second , some are in a wait status . Based on a variety of factors ( shared memory , library dependencies , file associations , registry keys ) , a specific OS may not be designed to schedule program removals at the same time .
