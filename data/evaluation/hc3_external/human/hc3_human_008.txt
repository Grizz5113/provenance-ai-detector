The future . Examples of borrowing from the future : 1 . A bank holds 10 dollars and loans out 100 under the expectation that not more than 10 dollars will be demanded by its customers at any time ( fractional reserve banking ) . 2 . A tech company IPO 's and its stock price rises ; derivatives ( fancy word for something whose price depends on something else ) based on it rise in value . 3 . Desperate for steel it needs to import during war , Germany prints a shitton of Reichsmarks . Each of these situations creates an expectation that value will exist in the future , an expectation that may or may not be borne out . " Money " is the quantitative measure of this collective expectation of future value . " Interest rates " are the first derivative of value ie . our best estimate for how fast value will increase .
