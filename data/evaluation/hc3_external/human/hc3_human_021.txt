Vowels are the " linking " sounds that connect other shorter consonant letter sounds together . DNGR is just a mash of tight quick sounds , but if we add some vowels it becomes DANGER . The vowels indicate the correct sound to link the consonants together . That 's why vowels are generally long noises , and not short quick sounds like consonants are . They are for linking other things together . There are plenty of other older written languages that lacked any vowels , just consonants , and you just needed to learn naturally the correct vowel sound to link those written consonants together . I know that s overly simplified , but that 's the gist of it .
