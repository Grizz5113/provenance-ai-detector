So basically a solar panel is made of many , many photovoltaic cells . Each cell is composed of a Cathode and an Anode inside of a vacuum pipe . Each anode is connected to a power source and a very sensitive current meter . When photons ( light ) hit the cell they " push " electrons off of the cathode and because anode is connected to a constant power source it attracts those electrons . When said electrons hit the anode the current changes and the surplus ( difference between constant current and when electrons hit the anode ) can be stored in accumulators .
