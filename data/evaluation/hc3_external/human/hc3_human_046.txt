Actually , for timekeeping purposes , we use the cesium atoms ' vibrations in the atomic clock as * * THE * * definition of the correct time . We set all our clocks to that one clock . It also keeps track of smaller units of time than it displays , since cesium atoms vibrate at exactly 9,192,631,770 Hz . We know the time down to one nine - millionth of a second . And , as for a smaller unit of time - yes , they exist , up until a point . The speed of light is the fastest something can go , and a plank distance is the shortest distance that something can move in the universe ( like the universe 's pixels if it were a screen ) . One plank time is the time it takes light to cross a plank distance ; in other words , you literally can not have a time shorter than a plank time , and there is no possible way to measure individual plank times .
