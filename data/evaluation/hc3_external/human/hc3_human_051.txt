Surface Waves ( After shock ) Surface waves are seismic waves that are guided along the surface of the Earth and the layers near the surface . Thus they have the greatest impact These waves do not penetrate the deep interior of the earth , Surface waves are larger in amplitude and longer in duration than body waves .
