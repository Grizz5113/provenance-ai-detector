In a normal military technique for getting infantry somewhere more quickly , at the cost of fatigue , reducing fighting ability , and higher casualties . They basically order the troops to walk farther and/or faster than they normally would , and threaten punishment for insubordination ( or worse ) if they do n't . Plus , if you have all the food , shelter and bullets and are somewhere people are trying to kill you , your soldiers have a pretty big incentive to keep up . You are typically going to also make allowances for more people getting hurt and find a way to transport them or safely leave them behind . Some forced marches in history have been much more brutal than that , unwilling , untrained , unequipped conscripts under threat of being shot if they do n't keep up .
