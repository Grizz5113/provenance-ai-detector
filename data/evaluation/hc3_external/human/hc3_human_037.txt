Rather , they refer to * degrees of freedom * , otherwise known as free variables . But you could also have " dimensions " like time , temperature , color , or anything else that you could change at will . However , if you 're talking about String Theory , they are indeed talking about something very like our spacial dimensions . So why ca n't we observe or interact with them ? Because they are tiny ! It 's like an ant farm ; the ants ca n't move toward or away from the viewer because there 's just not enough room between the glass . They are only barely big enough for things to squeeze into at the subatomic level .
