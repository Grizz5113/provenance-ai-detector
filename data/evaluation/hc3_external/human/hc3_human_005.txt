It will fix a tension pneumothorax . That is basically a hole in the lung causing air to escape through the hole and fill the space around the lung . The lung then can not continue to expand . Putting the hollow tube in allows the air around the lung to escape . You have to make it a one way valve though . The lung can then expand again . There is also a hemothorax which is blood filling around the lung . For that you need a chest tube to drain the fluid . Edit to add more info . If you have a tension pneumo , as you breath the air continues to fill the space around the lung . Then lung itself will stop expanding but the space around it will keep expanding .
