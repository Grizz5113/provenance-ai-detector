It 's actually not a one - way mirror , it 's just a semi - transparent glass . It reflects a certain part of light back , no matter which side you 're looking from . But then how does it work as a mirror , you may ask ? Well , if one room is really bright and one is really dark , then the glass will reflect 50 % of the light from each of them back inside . In a dark room there is no light , so there 's really nothing to reflect . As a result , you do n't see reflections . Meanwhile , in the bright room there 's a lot of light . If the glass reflects 50 % of it ( or 80 % , I dunno what 's the standard ) , then that 's more than enough light to make the glass look like a mirror . However , part of the light still goes through , so the people sitting in the dark room can still see you . Now if the brightness in both rooms was the same , then it would be simply a slightly tinted glass , that 's all .
