Preference .... honestly most modern operating systems are pretty similar over all , if anything the only down side to owning a mac is that if a game is going to be released on it its going to take longer and probably be buggy as shit as it normally gets outsourced to small developers ... the hardware is a little bit more expensive , but it very very very rarely has the same driver compatibility issues windows , or linux , or whatever other os you are using , has . This is because macs are designed for only a very select group of hardware , so they ' work better ' because they are more restrictive . there is a bit of a cultural component to owning a mac , lots of people complain about them not being real computers , but they are . You can do pretty much anything on a mac you can do in linux , and I doubt any geek will say a linux box is nt the way to go if you want total control . Then again , macs tend to be designed around the idea of ' it just works ' so they try to hide anything that a user might hurt themselves with . So you are of that personality type where you need to be in total control of your box , well , macs might not be a choice unless you really know how they work ....
