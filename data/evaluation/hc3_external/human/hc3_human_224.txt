In simplistic terms , it is running apps and using storage over the Internet , rather than your local device . You access it as a terminal , usually over a web browser . The benefit is that you do not need to buy expensive software , you can rent it or pay for it as you need it , and can access it from a number of devices and locations . The same for cloud storage . The downside is if that company goes away , or you do n't pay your subscription , you may lose your data , if you have n't backed it up elsewhere .
