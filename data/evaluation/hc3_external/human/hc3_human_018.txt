A while back , a person being sworn in would be asked something like this : " do you * swear * or * affirm * under the threat of [ perjury ] ( URL_0 ) that the testimony you provide will the truth , the whole truth , and nothing but the truth ? " They are asking if the person is going to be completely truthful and forthcoming , based on the threat of punishment from the courts . People who believe in God and would like to be sworn in on a bible have the option , typically because of the ninth commandment ( not bearing false witness ) . Nowadays , in a courtroom situation , they usually ask people to just raise their right hand . For something like the President , they typically go with the bible route to stick with tradition , appease religious voters / citizens , and because every President so far has been Christian .
