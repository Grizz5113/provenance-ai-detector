Your brain is an information - processing machine , and " pain " is one of the types of information your body deals in . For example , a paper cut on my finger releases certain molecules that excited nerves in my finger . These nerves then pass an electrochemical signal along to nerves in my spinal cord , which in turn passes that signal along to a part of my brain called the thalamus , which is where pain is perceived . Migraines are characterized by a severe widening of blood vessels in the brain and the release of pain molecules from the brain stem , which sends signals to the thalamus that your head really really hurts .
