You body uses most of your calories just to do the basic things it needs to do to stay alive . If you do n't get enough thyroid hormones , your body does those things less , you do n't burn enough calories , and you become fat , cold , weak and sleepy , even if you 're not eating more food . If your body releases too many thyroid hormones , your body burns extra calories and you become thin , twitchy , sweaty and anxious , even if you eat the same or more food . Tl;dr : Calories in , calories out . You thyroid controls how many calories go out .
