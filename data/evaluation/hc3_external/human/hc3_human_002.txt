Under ideal conditions , a tube & a transistor work exactly the same but the transistor is more reliable and cheaper . Transistors actually produce " cleaner " sound when they 're operated within their normal limits . The big difference come in when the components are pushed too hard and start distorting the signals . People generally feel that tube distortion gives the sound a " warm " feeling while an overdriven transistor is harsh & unpleasant . People have gotten used to the sound of a tube amp & it 's considered part of the " rock and roll sound " . These days , we do n't really * need * to use tubes - we can effectively use high quality solid state ( transistor ) amplifiers & digitally simulate the tube distortion . Artists are weird creatures & sometimes chose to stick to the old ways even if they 're not really better .
