The states distribute money to towns . The Fed gives it to the states . So , the State legislature determines what towns in their state get the money . States calculate how much they will make on each dollar they spend on a town ( in taxes over time , etc ) . An example for this is road maintenance . How much will a town make on road maintenance over time from assisting economy and taxes rather than letting a road rot ? A lot of road maintenance costs more to maintain than provides economy because of the spread of suburbanization and the network required to maintain those roads , which eventually makes counties broke as well . One of the major trends in urbanization is to reduce road maintenance costs to cities have more money to spend on other important things . Road maintenance can be said to be a major part in bankrupting towns and cities .
