I 'm not a chemist myself , but remember researching this topic when my aunt gave me a rechargeable one for christmas years ago Rechargeable hand warmers usually work with crystallization enthalpy . They contain some fluid material that would crystallize and become solid at room temperature but can not form the first crystal which acts as the starting point for the whole process When you bend the little metal plate inside you create a little wave that starts it anyway There is an " energy gap " between the fluid and the crystallized form that gets released in form of heat in the process When heating them up to recharge them you provide enough energy to bridge this " gap " and the chemical inside gets back into its fluid state until it can crystallize again
