Chopsticks and a spoon is basically everything you need to eat a meal . In Chinese cuisine , each person gets their own bowl of rice and all the dishes are bite - sized and located in the centre of the table . You grab what you like . There 's no portions or servings . It 's okay to take the bowl off the table and hold it in your hands as well as shovelling that last bit of rice at the end . It 's about enjoying yourself and having a good atmosphere rather than formalities and etiquette . That being said , try not to make a mess . Stand up , lean over with your bowl next to the dish so you can transfer without dripping sauce all over the table . For things like prawns and lobsters , it is acceptable to use your hands to remove the shell . Also , everyone knows how to use knives and forks too .
