There are three major ways an air bubble ( medically known as an air embolism ) can kill you : 1 . Your Heart . A large air embolism blocks an artery or vein in your heart and either prevents the chambers of the heart from filling up with blood to pump out or blocks the other end of the circuit and causes blood to continually build up without being expelled . 2 . Your Lungs . The embolism gets stuck in the arteries or veins that flow blood into and out of your lungs . In this case , even if you 're breathing the blood ca n't your lungs to be oxygenated . The tissue in your body requires oxygen to function and if it does n't receive it , you will die . 3 . Your Brain . The embolism blocks blood flow to and from your brain . Without blood flow ( and thus , oxygen ) your brain tissue dies . This is what a stroke is . Blood clots also act the same way as an air embolism , but in that case instead of an air bubble , it 's a big hunk of coagulated ( solid ) blood cells . TL;DR : the air blocks blood flow to very important areas of your body
