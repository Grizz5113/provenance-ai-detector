First , what kind of bank , second , what form of money ? Where do they keep cash ? In vaults at bank locations . They either get it from customers or have it delivered by cash services ( armored trucks ) . In truth , there might be a few million in cash at a branch during the day , but even all that money put together is nowhere near the bank 's real value . Most of the money that banks have is actually debt . They borrow money from bigger banks or the US Federal Reserve and loan it out at a higher interest rate than the loaner is charging . Most of the money itself is " on paper " meaning the value is theirs but they do n't have all of it in real hard cash .
