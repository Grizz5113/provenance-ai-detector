Cameras capture their scenes by opening a shutter for a period of time , exposing a film or image sensor to capture information about the scene . Each frame represents a period of time and moving objects will be shown as a blurry smear across the frame . Computer renderings are made of a calculated theoretical instant . As a single instant there can be no motion and moving objects are as clear as if stationary , and are only in one place . So you see a frame from a computer rendering contains less information than that of a camera , and the disconnects make it look jerky . A normal camera at least connects the ends of motion blurs while the rendered frames just jump from place to place .
