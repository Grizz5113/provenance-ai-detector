There have been * extensive * studies done on the subject , and the NTSB ( National Transportation Safety Board , the federal agency in charge of highway safety ) is entirely on board with the no - seat - belt thing . Public transport busses basically work the same way . But liability relating to the * design of the vehicle * would fall on the manufacturer , not the district . And as the design is widely known to be at least as reasonably safe as a belted design , that 's just a non - starter . Believe me , if there was * any * reason to think that seat belts are safer than what we 're doing now , every school bus in the country would have them .
