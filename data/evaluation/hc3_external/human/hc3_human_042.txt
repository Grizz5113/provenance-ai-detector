As the others have stated , it 's for water pressure , but it 's also for efficiency . Using a water tower , you need a pump capable of lifting a day 's worth of water in a day , rather than following the demand curves . Well , you need more capacity for reserve . so it needs to be capable of pumping more than a day 's worth of water per day . This means you can use a fairly efficient single - speed pump , as opposed to needing a less efficient variable - speed pump that needs to pump more at times of higher demand and less at times of lower demand . So using a water tower will be cheaper overall than just using pumps to keep the system pressurized . Plus there 's the added benefit of not needing nearly as robust a backup power source , since the pressure in the system wo n't go down just because there 's a temporary power outage .
