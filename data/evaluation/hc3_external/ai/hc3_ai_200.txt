It is possible that swelling due to wisdom tooth eruption can be managed with measures such as warm salt water rinses and over-the-counter pain medication, but in most cases, extraction is the most effective way to manage the problem. If the wisdom tooth is causing discomfort or is coming in at an angle that is causing pain or affecting your other teeth, extraction is likely the best course of action. If you are concerned about the extraction process, it is important to discuss your concerns with your dentist or oral surgeon so that they can address your concerns and help you feel more comfortable. They may be able to offer options such as sedation or pain management to make the procedure more comfortable for you.
