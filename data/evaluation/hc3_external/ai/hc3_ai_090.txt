Whether you should file taxes or incorporate your personal project depends on a number of factors, including the nature of your project, your financial situation, and your personal goals. Here are a few things to consider:If your personal project generates income, you will need to file taxes on that income. This is true regardless of whether you incorporate your project or not.Incorporating your personal project can provide certain legal and financial benefits, such as limited liability protection and potential tax advantages. However, incorporation also involves additional costs and administrative responsibilities, such as filing articles of incorporation and maintaining corporate records.If you are considering incorporating your personal project, it may be helpful to consult with a tax professional or an attorney to understand the potential benefits and drawbacks of incorporation and to determine the best course of action for your specific situation.Ultimately, the decision of whether to file taxes or incorporate your personal project will depend on your individual circumstances and goals. It may be helpful to carefully consider your options and seek guidance from a professional before making a decision.
