Electrical plugs with two prongs have a certain orientation because it helps to ensure that they are inserted into the socket in the correct way. This is important because electricity is flowing through the wires inside the plug, and we want to make sure that it is flowing in the right direction.\n\nImagine that you have a garden hose with water flowing through it. If you put the hose into a spigot the wrong way, the water won't come out. It's the same with electricity - if the plug is inserted the wrong way, the electricity won't flow correctly and it could cause problems with the device you are trying to use.\n\nBy making the plugs a certain shape, it helps us to know which way to insert them so that the electricity flows correctly.
