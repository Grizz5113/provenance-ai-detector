It is possible that the tightness you are experiencing around your chest while eating could be due to a condition called esophageal spasm. Esophageal spasm is a disorder of the muscle contractions in the esophagus, the tube that carries food from the mouth to the stomach. It can cause symptoms such as chest pain, difficulty swallowing, and the feeling of food getting stuck in the throat.

There are several possible causes of esophageal spasm, including acid reflux, a hiatal hernia, and abnormal nerve or muscle function in the esophagus. Consuming hot food may exacerbate the symptoms of esophageal spasm, but it is not the sole cause of the condition.

It is important to discuss your symptoms with a medical professional to determine the cause and appropriate treatment. They may recommend medications or other treatments to help alleviate the symptoms of esophageal spasm. In the meantime, it may be helpful to avoid hot or spicy foods, as well as eating large meals, as these can contribute to the symptoms of the condition.
