Humanities are academic disciplines that study the human condition, using methods that are primarily analytical, critical, or speculative, as opposed to the empirical methods of the natural sciences. The humanities include ancient and modern languages, literature, philosophy, history, archaeology, anthropology, human geography, law, politics, and art.\n\nSome examples of specific classes that might be considered part of the humanities include:\n\nArt history\nClassics (ancient Greek and Roman civilization, language, and literature)\nComparative literature\nCultural studies\nHistory\nLinguistics\nLiterature (in various languages and from different periods)\nPhilosophy\nReligious studies\nAnthropology\nIt's worth noting that the exact boundaries of what subjects are considered part of the humanities can vary depending on the context and the specific institution. Some disciplines, like sociology or psychology, may be considered part of the humanities in some contexts, but not in others.\n
