Banks keep money in a few different places. First, they keep some of their money in their own vaults, which are like very safe rooms where they keep cash and other valuable items. This is the money that customers can come in and withdraw from the bank. Banks also keep some of their money in other banks, called "correspondent banks." They do this so that they have access to more cash if they need it. Finally, banks also keep some of their money in investments, like stocks and bonds. These investments can help them make more money, but they also come with some risk because the value of the investments can go up or down. Overall, banks try to keep a balance between keeping enough cash on hand to meet the needs of their customers and investing some of their money to make more money.
