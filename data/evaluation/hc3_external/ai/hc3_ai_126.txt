It is possible that the use of Yasmin (ethinyl estradiol and drospirenone) may contribute to the development of thrush or a yeast infection. Hormonal contraceptives, including oral contraceptives like Yasmin, can alter the normal balance of bacteria in the vagina, which can lead to an overgrowth of Candida, a type of fungus that can cause thrush or a yeast infection.

If you are experiencing symptoms of thrush or a yeast infection and you have recently started taking Yasmin, it is important to speak with your healthcare provider. They can help determine whether the medication may be contributing to your symptoms and provide you with appropriate treatment. In the meantime, it is important to follow your healthcare provider's instructions for using any prescribed treatment, such as Canestan cream, and to practice good hygiene to help prevent the spread of the infection.
