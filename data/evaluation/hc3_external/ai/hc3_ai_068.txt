There are a few ways that a public company can turn its shares into cash:Sell the shares: A company can sell its shares on a stock exchange to investors who are interested in buying them. The company will receive cash in exchange for the shares.Buyback the shares: A company can also buy back its own shares from investors on the open market. This is known as a "share buyback." The company will pay cash for the shares it buys back, which can then be retired or held as treasury stock.Issue dividends: Another way for a company to turn its shares into cash is by issuing dividends. Dividends are payments made by the company to shareholders, typically in the form of cash or additional shares.Raise capital through new equity: Finally, a company can raise capital by issuing new shares of stock and selling them to investors. This can be done through a public offering or a private placement. The company will receive cash in exchange for the new shares it issues.
