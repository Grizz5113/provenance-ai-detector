Doctors ask the same questions that you told the nurse before they came in to make sure they have a complete and accurate understanding of your medical history and current condition. It's important for doctors to have as much information as possible when they are diagnosing and treating patients.

Think of it like a puzzle: the nurse gathers some of the pieces, and the doctor adds more pieces to the puzzle to get a clearer picture. Both the nurse and the doctor play important roles in your care, and both need to have all the necessary information to make sure you get the best treatment possible.

It might seem annoying to have to repeat yourself, but it's actually a very important part of the medical process. It helps doctors make sure they have all the information they need to help you feel better.
