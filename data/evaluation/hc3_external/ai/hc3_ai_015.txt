Gentrification can be a controversial topic because it can change the character and makeup of a neighborhood. When wealthier people move into a poorer area, they often bring with them new businesses and improvements to houses and other buildings. This can make the neighborhood a better place to live, but it can also make it more expensive for the people who already live there. If the people who have lived in the neighborhood for a long time can't afford to stay, they may have to move away. This can be hard for them because they may have to leave their friends, schools, and other parts of their community behind.
