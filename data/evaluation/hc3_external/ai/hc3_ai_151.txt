Lumps near the groin can have a variety of causes, including swollen lymph nodes, hernias, and other conditions. It is important to have any new lump or growth checked by a healthcare provider in order to determine the cause and appropriate treatment.

Swollen lymph nodes are a common cause of lumps in the groin area. Lymph nodes are part of the immune system and can become swollen in response to infection or inflammation. Straining, masturbation, and irritation are not likely to cause swollen lymph nodes, but certain activities such as heavy lifting or straining to have a bowel movement could potentially cause a hernia.

A hernia is a protrusion of an organ or tissue through a weak point in the muscle or surrounding tissue. Hernias can occur in the groin area and can cause a lump or swelling. In some cases, a hernia may be accompanied by discomfort or pain, especially when coughing, laughing, or lifting heavy objects.
