Real estate agents typically earn a commission for their work in helping people buy and sell properties. When a person buys a property using a mortgage, the agent may receive a commission from the seller of the property. This commission is typically a percentage of the sale price of the property. For example, let's say that a person buys a house for $300,000, and the real estate agent's commission is 5%. The agent would earn a commission of $15,000, which would be paid by the seller of the house. Real estate agents also may earn commissions from the lender or mortgage broker when they help someone get a mortgage. This commission may be based on the size of the loan or the type of mortgage product that the buyer chooses. Overall, real estate agents earn money by helping people buy and sell properties and by assisting with the financing of those properties.
