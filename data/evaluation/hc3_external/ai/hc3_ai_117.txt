It is generally not necessary to rebalance a 401k with only one index fund, since the purpose of rebalancing is to maintain a desired asset allocation among the investments in your portfolio. If you only have one index fund in your 401k, then your portfolio is already fully allocated to that fund and there is nothing to rebalance.However, it is still a good idea to periodically review your 401k and make sure that it is aligned with your investment goals and risk tolerance. This might involve adjusting your contribution rate, changing the investments in your portfolio, or both. It is also a good idea to periodically review your investment allocation to make sure that it is still appropriate for your situation. For example, as you get closer to retirement, you may want to shift your portfolio to a more conservative allocation to protect against market volatility.
