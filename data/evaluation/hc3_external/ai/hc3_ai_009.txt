When an object is heated, it can emit light. The color of the light depends on the temperature of the object. At low temperatures, an object may appear red, because it is emitting mostly red light. As the temperature increases, the color of the light can change. At high temperatures, an object may appear blue or white, because it is emitting more short-wavelength light. The video you mentioned is talking about something called blackbody radiation, which is a type of electromagnetic radiation that is emitted by an object that is at a high temperature. According to the theory of blackbody radiation, if an object is heated to a high enough temperature, it will eventually start to emit light with very short wavelengths, such as ultraviolet light and X-rays. As the temperature increases even further, the object may start to emit light with even shorter wavelengths, such as gamma rays. This light is not visible to the human eye, which is why we don't see it.
