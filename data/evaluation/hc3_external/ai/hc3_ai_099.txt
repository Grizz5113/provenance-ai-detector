USDA loans are a type of mortgage offered by the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) to help low- and moderate-income individuals and families buy, repair, or improve homes in rural areas. To be eligible for a USDA loan, you must meet certain income and credit requirements, as well as the property you wish to purchase must be located in an eligible rural area. In addition, you must be a U.S. citizen or a qualified noncitizen, and you must be able to afford the monthly mortgage payments, including taxes and insurance.To determine if you are eligible for a USDA loan, you will need to provide information about your income, employment, debts, and assets. The USDA will then use this information to calculate your debt-to-income ratio, which must be within their guidelines in order for you to qualify.If you are interested in applying for a USDA loan, you should contact a USDA-approved lender, who can help you determine your eligibility and assist you with the application process.
