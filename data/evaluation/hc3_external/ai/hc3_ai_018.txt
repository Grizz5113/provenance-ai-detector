Swearing on the Bible in a legal situation, also known as taking an oath, is a way for a person to promise to tell the truth. The idea is that by swearing on something that is sacred or meaningful to them, they will be more likely to keep their promise. In the past, the Bible was often used for this purpose because it is a holy book for many people, especially in countries with a Christian tradition.

Today, people who do not follow a particular religion or do not believe in God may also be asked to swear on the Bible or to affirm that they will tell the truth. In these cases, the act of swearing on the Bible may not have the same religious significance, but it is still a way to show that a person is serious about telling the truth.

In some legal situations, people may also be asked to swear on other objects that are meaningful to them, such as a religious text from a different religion or a personal object like a family heirloom. The important thing is that the person is making a sincere promise to tell the truth.
