A companion fare is a discounted fare that is offered to a passenger traveling with someone else, usually a friend or family member. The terms and conditions of a companion fare will vary depending on the airline and the specific fare offer, but generally, the main passenger will pay the full fare for their ticket, while the companion will pay a discounted fare.If the total cost of the fare for both passengers exceeds the companion fare limit, you will need to pay the difference between the discounted companion fare and the full fare. For example, if the full fare for a ticket is $500 and the companion fare limit is $300, the main passenger would pay $500 and the companion would pay the difference between the full fare and the companion fare limit, which would be $200 in this case.To use a companion fare, you will need to book the tickets together and make sure to enter the companion fare code when prompted during the booking process. The exact steps for using a companion fare will vary depending on the airline and the booking process, so it is a good idea to check with the airline directly or refer to the terms and conditions of the companion fare offer for more information.
