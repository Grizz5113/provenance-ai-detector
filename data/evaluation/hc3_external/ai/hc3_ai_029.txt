Genetics are the instructions that tell your body how to grow and function. These instructions are stored in your DNA, which is like a recipe book that is passed down from your parents to you.\n\nYour DNA is made up of many different genes, and each gene has a specific job to do. Some genes are responsible for things like your eye color, your height, and your hair color. Other genes are responsible for things like your metabolism, which is how your body uses food for energy.\n\nYour genes are passed down to you from your parents, and you get half of your genes from your mother and half from your father. This means that you have some of the same genes as your mom, but you also have some different genes from your dad.\n\nSo, if your mom is heavy set, that means that she has certain genes that make it more likely for her to be heavy. If you are active and fit, that means that you have different genes or you have the same genes but they are expressed differently. It's also possible that your lifestyle, including your diet and exercise habits, can have an impact on your weight and overall health.\n\nIn short, genetics play a role in how your body looks and functions, but they are not the only factor. Your lifestyle and environment can also have an impact on your health and well-being.
