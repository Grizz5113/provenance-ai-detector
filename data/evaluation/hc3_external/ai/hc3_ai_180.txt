It is difficult to accurately diagnose a skin condition without seeing it in person or having more information about it. It is important to have your child evaluated by a healthcare provider for an accurate diagnosis and appropriate treatment. There are many different conditions that can cause red patches on the skin, including eczema, seborrheic dermatitis, and various infections. A healthcare provider will be able to examine your child and determine the cause of the rash and recommend the appropriate treatment. In the meantime, you can try keeping the affected area clean and dry and applying a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer to the skin to help soothe any irritation. Avoiding irritants such as harsh soaps or detergents and wearing loose, comfortable clothing may also be helpful.
