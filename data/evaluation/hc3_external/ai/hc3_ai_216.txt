Thunder is the sound that you hear when lightning strikes. Lightning is a very bright flash of electricity that travels through the air. When lightning strikes, it can cause the air to vibrate, and that vibration creates the sound of thunder.Sometimes, when lightning strikes, the air doesn't vibrate very much, so the thunder is not very loud and doesn't last very long. Other times, the lightning is much more powerful and makes the air vibrate more, so the thunder is louder and lasts longer.There are also other factors that can affect how long thunder lasts. For example, if the lightning strikes something far away, the thunder might be quieter and not last as long because the sound has to travel a longer distance.
