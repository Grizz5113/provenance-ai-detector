Modifying an order typically cancels the old order and submits a new one with the modified details. This is done so that the new modified order can be processed and filled separately from the original order. However, the specifics of how order modifications are handled can vary depending on the exchange or trading platform you are using. Some exchanges or platforms may allow you to modify an order without canceling the original order, but this is not always the case. It's a good idea to check with the exchange or platform you are using to understand how they handle order modifications.
