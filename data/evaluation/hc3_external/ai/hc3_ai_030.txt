Ayn Rand was a writer and philosopher who is known for her ideas about individualism and capitalism. Some people hate her because they disagree with her ideas about selfishness being a virtue, and they think her views are overly simplified and naive. Other people might hate her because of the way she presents her ideas, which can be very dogmatic and absolutist. Still, others might simply find her writing style unappealing or find her personality off-putting. It's important to remember that not everyone has to agree with someone else's ideas, and it's okay to dislike someone's work without hating them as a person.
