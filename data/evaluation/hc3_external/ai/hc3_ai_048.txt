350 degrees Fahrenheit is a standard cooking temperature because it is a temperature that is hot enough to cook most foods, but not so hot that it will burn them. Different foods require different cooking temperatures to turn out well. For example, some foods need to be cooked at a higher temperature to brown or crisp up, while others need a lower temperature to cook evenly without burning. 350 degrees Fahrenheit is a good middle ground that works well for a wide range of recipes. It's not really a "magic number," it's just a temperature that has been found to be suitable for many types of food.
