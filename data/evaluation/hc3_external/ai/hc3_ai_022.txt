Great question! Having more cylinders in a motor can give it more power because each cylinder can produce power independently. In a motor with only one cylinder, all of the power for the motor has to come from that one cylinder. But in a motor with more cylinders, each cylinder can produce a small amount of power, and when all of the cylinders are working together, they can produce more power overall. So even though each cylinder in an 8-cylinder motor might be smaller than each cylinder in a 6-cylinder motor, the 8-cylinder motor can still produce more power because it has more cylinders working together. Does that make sense?
