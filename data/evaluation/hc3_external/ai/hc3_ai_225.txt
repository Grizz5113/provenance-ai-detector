Thyroid problems can cause weight gain because the thyroid gland helps regulate your body's metabolism, which is the process that your body uses to turn food into energy. If your thyroid gland is not working properly, it can slow down your metabolism, which means that your body may not be able to burn calories as efficiently as it should. This can lead to weight gain, even if you are eating the same amount of calories as before. So, while calories are still important, they are not the only factor that can affect your weight. Your body's metabolism and hormonal balance can also play a role.
