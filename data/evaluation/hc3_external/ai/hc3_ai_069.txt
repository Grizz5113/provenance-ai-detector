Banks generally do not intentionally allow transactions without sufficient funds. Rather, they may have policies in place that allow certain types of transactions to be processed even if they result in an overdraft of the account. This can be done as a courtesy to the account holder, with the understanding that the account will be brought back into good standing by depositing additional funds or by paying off the overdraft.There may be fees associated with overdrafts, and the account holder is responsible for paying those fees as well as bringing the account back into good standing. It is important for account holders to understand their bank's policies regarding overdrafts and to manage their accounts responsibly to avoid incurring fees or other negative consequences.
