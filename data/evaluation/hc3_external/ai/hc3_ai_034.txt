An electric car can power itself, as it uses electricity stored in a battery to turn the wheels and move the car. The battery is charged by plugging the car into an electric outlet or a charging station.\n\nIt is possible to design a system that uses the motion of the wheels to generate electricity and recharge the battery, but this would not be very efficient. The energy needed to turn the wheels and move the car comes from the battery, so using some of this energy to power a generator would reduce the amount of power available to move the car. This would make the car go slower and shorten the distance it can travel on a single charge.\n\nOverall, it is more efficient to use a separate source of electricity (such as a plug or a charging station) to recharge the battery, rather than trying to generate electricity while the car is moving.
