In the world of Thomas and Friends, Sir Topham Hatt is the "Fat Controller," which means he is in charge of all the trains on the island of Sodor. The trains in Thomas and Friends are animated characters, and they are able to understand and communicate with Sir Topham Hatt and with each other.\n\nEven though there are real people who operate and maintain the trains in the real world, in the world of Thomas and Friends, the trains can think and talk on their own. Sir Topham Hatt talks to the trains to give them instructions, praise them for their hard work, or scold them if they have done something wrong.\n\nIt might seem strange to us that Sir Topham Hatt is talking to trains, but in the world of Thomas and Friends, this is just how things work.
