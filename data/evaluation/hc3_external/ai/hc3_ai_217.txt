Outlet stores often have lower prices than regular stores for a few reasons.\n\nFirst, outlet stores typically sell items that are from previous seasons or that didn't sell as well in regular stores. These items might be discounted because they are no longer the most current styles, or because there is less demand for them.\n\nSecond, outlet stores often have lower overhead costs than regular stores. For example, they might be located in less expensive areas or they might not have as many employees. These lower costs can allow them to sell items at lower prices.\n\nFinally, outlet stores often buy items in bulk or directly from the manufacturer, which can help them get a better price. They can then pass these savings on to customers by selling the items at a lower price.\n\nOverall, outlet stores offer lower prices because they sell items that are not as in demand and because they have lower costs.
