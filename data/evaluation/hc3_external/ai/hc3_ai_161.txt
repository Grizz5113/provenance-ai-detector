It is possible for acid reflux to cause an increase in heart rate. Acid reflux, also known as gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), is a condition in which stomach acid flows back up into the esophagus (the tube that connects the mouth to the stomach). This can cause symptoms such as heartburn, chest pain, and difficulty swallowing.

In some cases, acid reflux can also lead to other symptoms, including a rapid heart rate. This may occur because the acid irritates the esophagus and can lead to inflammation, which can affect the functioning of the vagus nerve. The vagus nerve helps regulate the heart rate, and when it is irritated or inflamed, it can cause the heart to beat faster.

It is important to note that a rapid heart rate can also be caused by other factors, such as stress, anxiety, physical activity, or certain medications. If you are experiencing a rapid heart rate, it is important to speak with a healthcare provider to determine the cause and receive appropriate treatment.
