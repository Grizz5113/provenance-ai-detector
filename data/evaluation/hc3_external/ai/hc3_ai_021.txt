Vowels are different from other sounds in that they are made by allowing air to flow freely through the mouth, without any obstruction. This makes them sound different from other sounds, which are made by blocking or restricting the flow of air in some way.

Vowels are important in language because they help us distinguish one word from another. For example, the word "bat" has a different meaning than the word "bet," and the only difference between them is the vowel sound in the middle. Without vowels, it would be much harder to tell words apart, and our language would not work as well.
