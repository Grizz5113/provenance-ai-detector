New laws are passed by governments, which can be at the local, state, or national level. Governments make new laws for many reasons, such as to protect the safety and well-being of their citizens, to promote fairness and justice, or to address problems in society.It is not always possible for every person to know about every new law that is being passed. However, it is important for people to be aware of the laws that apply to them, especially if they could potentially be breaking those laws. For example, if you are driving a car, you should know the traffic laws in your area so that you don't accidentally break them.If you do violate a law, you may be punished in some way, such as by paying a fine or going to jail. In most cases, the punishment will depend on the seriousness of the violation and the laws of the place where it happened.To stay informed about new laws, you can read about them in the news, ask people who work in government or law enforcement, or look for information on the internet or at your local library. You can also ask a lawyer or legal professional for advice if you have questions about specific laws or how they might affect you.
