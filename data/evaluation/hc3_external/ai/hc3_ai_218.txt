Sure! When you spill a glass of water on your floor, the water starts to spread out and cover more of the floor. The water molecules are very tiny and can move around easily. As the water touches the floor, it starts to evaporate, which means that the water turns from a liquid into a gas. When water evaporates, it turns into water vapor, which is a type of gas. The water vapor rises into the air and floats around until it finds something cold to touch. When it touches something cold, the water vapor turns back into a liquid and forms tiny droplets on the surface. This is called condensation. So, when you spill water on your floor and it disappears, it has actually just evaporated into the air and turned into water vapor. The water vapor has then risen up into the air and turned back into tiny droplets of water on the cold surface.
