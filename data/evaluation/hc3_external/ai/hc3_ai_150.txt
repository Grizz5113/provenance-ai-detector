Swelling of the eyelid after heart surgery can have a variety of causes, including allergies, infection, or a reaction to medications. It is important to speak with a healthcare provider about any unusual symptoms following surgery, as they can indicate a potential problem.

It is possible that the swelling of the eyelid could be related to the heart surgery, although this is not necessarily the case. It could also be a separate issue that is unrelated to the surgery.

If the swelling is severe or accompanied by other symptoms such as fever, difficulty breathing, or chest pain, it is important to seek medical attention immediately. In less severe cases, it may be sufficient to contact the healthcare provider who performed the surgery or the patient's primary care provider for further guidance on how to manage the symptom.
