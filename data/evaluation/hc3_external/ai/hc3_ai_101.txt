It can be difficult to know what to do when a trendline or pattern breaks, especially if the break is due to negative news. Here are a few things to consider:Assess the severity of the news: How significant is the negative news? Is it likely to have a long-term impact on the market or is it a temporary blip?Look for other indicators: Are there other technical indicators or fundamental factors that suggest the trendline or pattern may return?Consider the overall market trend: Is the market trend still generally positive, or has it turned negative?Use stop-loss orders: If you are holding a position and the trendline or pattern breaks, you may want to consider placing a stop-loss order to limit your potential losses.It's important to remember that markets can be volatile, and it's not uncommon for trendlines and patterns to break. It's always a good idea to diversify your portfolio and manage risk through techniques such as stop-loss orders.
