It is important to manage high blood pressure during pregnancy as it can have serious complications for both the mother and the baby. However, it is also important to avoid taking any medications that have not been prescribed by a healthcare provider during pregnancy, as some medications can be harmful to the developing baby.

If you are concerned about your blood pressure, you should contact your healthcare provider as soon as possible. Your healthcare provider will be able to assess your blood pressure and determine the best course of treatment for you. They may recommend lifestyle changes, such as increasing physical activity, reducing salt intake, and managing stress, to help lower your blood pressure. They may also prescribe medications that are safe to take during pregnancy, if necessary.

In the meantime, it is important to follow your healthcare provider's instructions and avoid taking any medications unless directed to do so. If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider for guidance.
