In the past, armies were often fed by foraging for food, which means they would search for food in the areas where they were fighting. This could include things like hunting for game, gathering fruits and vegetables, and raiding farms and stores for supplies.Armies also often carried their own supplies with them, such as grains, dried meats, and other nonperishable foods that could be stored for long periods of time. These supplies would be transported by carts, wagons, or pack animals, and would be replenished as needed by resupply convoys.In addition to these methods, armies sometimes also set up camp near a city or town, and the local population would be required to provide them with food and other supplies in exchange for protection.Overall, feeding an entire army was a difficult and complex task, and required careful planning and organization. It was especially challenging during long campaigns or sieges, when supplies could run low and it was difficult to restock.
