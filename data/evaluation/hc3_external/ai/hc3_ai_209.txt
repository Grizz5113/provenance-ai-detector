Absurdism and nihilism are similar in that they both recognize that life may lack inherent meaning. However, they differ in how they respond to this lack of meaning.Absurdism is the idea that the search for meaning in life is inherently flawed or impossible because the universe is inherently meaningless. However, this does not mean that an individual cannot find meaning in their own life. In fact, absurdists often believe that individuals have the freedom to create their own meaning and purpose in life, despite the universe's inherent lack of meaning.Nihilism, on the other hand, is the belief that life is meaningless and that there is no purpose or value in anything. Nihilists believe that life has no inherent meaning and that any meaning or purpose that individuals may try to find or create is ultimately futile.So, while both absurdism and nihilism recognize that life may lack inherent meaning, absurdism allows for the possibility of individuals creating their own meaning, while nihilism rejects the idea of any meaning at all.
