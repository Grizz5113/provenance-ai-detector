Sound is a type of energy that travels through the air or through other materials as a vibration. When an object vibrates, it moves back and forth quickly. This movement causes the air around it to vibrate too, and this vibration travels through the air as a sound wave.

When you flick a table, the movement of your finger causes the table to vibrate. This vibration travels through the table and into the air, creating a sound wave that we can hear.
