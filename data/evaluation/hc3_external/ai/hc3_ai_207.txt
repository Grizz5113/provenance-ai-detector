Toll roads are legal because they are a way for governments or private companies to fund the construction, maintenance, and operation of a road. Instead of paying for the road through taxes, drivers pay a fee, or toll, to use the road.

Using a fast pass or pike pass lane without having the pass is generally not allowed, and you could get a ticket for doing so. Think of it like a special lane at the grocery store for people who have a store loyalty card. If you don't have the card, you can't use the lane. Similarly, if you don't have a fast pass or pike pass, you can't use the special lane on the toll road.
