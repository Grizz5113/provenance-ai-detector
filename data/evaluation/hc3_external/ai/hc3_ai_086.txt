It is possible that you could be experiencing liver tenderness due to a variety of reasons, including liver inflammation, liver damage, or the presence of a mass or growth on the liver. It is important to have this symptom evaluated by a healthcare provider, as it could be a sign of a serious underlying condition that requires treatment. Some possible causes of liver tenderness include viral hepatitis, alcoholic liver disease, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, and liver cancer. Other possible causes could include pancreatitis, gallstones, or a kidney infection. It is important to speak with a healthcare provider to determine the cause of your liver tenderness and to determine the appropriate course of treatment.
