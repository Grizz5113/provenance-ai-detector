Solar panels work by using energy from the sun to create electricity. They do this by using materials that allow them to absorb sunlight and convert it into electricity.

Here's how it works: The sunlight hits the solar panels and is absorbed by the materials inside. These materials allow the energy from the sunlight to knock electrons loose, which creates a flow of electricity. This flow of electricity is then sent to an inverter, which converts the electricity into a form that can be used to power your home or business.

So, to sum it up, solar panels use the energy from the sun to create electricity, which can then be used to power your electronic devices.
