Dear Principal,

I strongly suggest allowing students to have their cell phones out during lunchtime and free time. They should definitely turn them off during class time, because we need to prioritize our education. During class time, if some students are simply paying attention to their phones, they wouldn’t get any learning done, or even pay attention to what the teachers are saying. I suggest that when coming into the classroom, students hand their phones over to the teacher or must show him or her turning them off; this would allow the teacher to know that the students are paying attention. Having your phone in class is disruptive. What if there’s a test coming up and the class is going over it, and a student isn't paying attention? That student could fail simply because of their phone. The only time they should have their phones throughout the day is during free time and lunch. There’s no learning being done then, so why not have them out? There really isn’t any harm in having your phone out during those times of the school day. If students show defiance or abuse the phone rule, the phone should be taken from that student. The students should receive three warnings: 1. Warning; 2. Taken until the end of class; and 3. The parent has to come get it. It’s good that you may allow us to have phones out, but if these rules are abused, you should restrict students from having phones in or at our school period.

Sincerely,

Student
