There are many reasons why canceled shows don't get restarted, even if there is demand for them. One reason is that it can be expensive to produce a TV show, and the production company or network may not think that they will make enough money to justify the cost. Another reason is that the actors, writers, and other people who worked on the show may have moved on to other projects and may not be available to work on the show again. Additionally, it can be difficult to get all of the rights to a show and to get everyone who was involved in the original production to agree to do a new version. Finally, even if a show is popular with fans, that doesn't necessarily mean that it will be successful if it is brought back, as tastes and interests can change over time.
