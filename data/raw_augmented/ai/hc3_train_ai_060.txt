If you have lent money to your son and are looking to get it back, there are a few steps you can take:Have a conversation with your son about the loan and your expectations for repayment. It's important to be clear and specific about when you expect the loan to be repaid and what the consequences will be if it is not repaid.Consider setting up a payment plan or schedule with your son to help him pay back the loan. This can help to ensure that the loan is repaid in a timely manner.If your son is unable or unwilling to repay the loan, you may need to consider legal action. This could involve hiring an attorney and potentially taking your son to court to get a judgment for the unpaid loan.Consider alternative methods of repayment, such as having your son perform work or provide services in lieu of making cash payments.It's important to approach this situation with patience and understanding, as it can be difficult to get money back from family members. However, it is also important to be firm and assertive in order to protect your financial interests.
