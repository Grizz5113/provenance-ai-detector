Dear Principal,

As a student within this school community, I recognize the concerns regarding cell phone usage and your consideration of revising the current policy. After reviewing both proposed options, I firmly believe that adopting the first policy – permitting students to bring phones and utilize them during lunch periods and other designated free times – represents the most beneficial approach.

My reasoning centers on the practical needs of students. There are occasions when a student requires immediate contact with their parents due to unforeseen circumstances; restricting phone access would unnecessarily interrupt communication and potentially cause anxiety for both the student and their family. Allowing phone use during these moments, outside of instructional time, ensures that students can maintain connection without compromising their education.

Conversely, I understand the rationale behind the second policy – prohibiting cell phones entirely within school grounds. However, I believe this approach could be detrimental in emergency situations where a student might require immediate assistance and the ability to quickly contact help. 

I sincerely hope this letter provides you with valuable insight into the perspectives of students regarding these proposed changes.
