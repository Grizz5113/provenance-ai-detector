Our principle has decided that all students must participate in one extracurricular activity. Students can serve as student council, work on the yearbook, or even play a sport. I think this is a good idea – or at least a way for kids to express themselves. Here are some reasons why I agree with being a part of these activities.

One thing you can do is work on the yearbook. You can also open up and help motivate others. Participating in something helps with many skills such as communication and working with others. The yearbook could even have anonymous writers, if you want to say something and don’t want everyone knowing who said it. Also, taking photos of other activities around the school would be great.

Another extracurricular activity you could do is serving as student council. If you want to see changes in something, you can step up and work as a council to make a difference. You could also get groups to hang motivational quotes around the school. Sometimes, even having funds to raise money for the school would be a helpful idea.

You can also play sports to participate in an activity. Playing sports gives you the time to make new friends, do what you love, and even hang out with your best friend. Finding a sport you like can be hard, but giving it your all is the best you can do. You can even get scholarships to colleges. And being on a team is your best support group; they are always there when you need them.

In conclusion, I agree we should participate in extracurricular activities. It gives us the opportunity to express ourselves and have fun doing it. They can also help try and change things to make a difference. The activities also have benefits for us and for school. All of these are reasons why we should participate in extracurricular activities.
