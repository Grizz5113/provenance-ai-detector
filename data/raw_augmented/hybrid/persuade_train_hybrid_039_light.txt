In my opinion, online schooling wouldn’t be good for a kid. Kids in public or private schools interact with other children all day, every day of the week, so it's normal for them to be social around peers. Also, kids who do online classes at home have more of a chance to feel awkward when around people since they don’t see people besides the video conferencing.

Parents might think homeschooling is better, but for the most part, it’s only hurting the kids’ social skills and they may not be fully understanding the material being taught. It’s been proven that children learn better around other kids and it especially helps when they are actually seeing what they are being taught. Kids learn a lot more together than alone; students in public or private schools develop the knowledge to learn and start friendships, field trips, recess, and best of all, field day.

Another reason I think online classes aren’t beneficial for students is because online classes require too much time staring at a computer screen and not enough interacting with real humans. All of this is more devastating to the brain than beneficial in my opinion. Kids just need other kids around to function sometimes, another person who can match their own energy 24/7. Students in homeschooling just won’t get the most exciting childhood in a sense.

In conclusion, kids who are in a public school or private school tend to be more social and more outgoing than kids in homeschooling; they also tend to learn more as a group and just have a better time learning.
