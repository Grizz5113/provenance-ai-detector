Dear Principal,

If you were to let us (kids) use our cell phones during lunch, I believe it would be beneficial. Another reason we should use them is because we can text our friends during lunch so we don’t have to wait until after school to contact them. During lunch, we can text our friends and, if we need to send a quick message to our mom just in case we forget our homework, we can text her to bring it for us before we receive a zero percent grade on the assignment.

We could also use our cell phones to take pictures of our homework assignments and share them with classmates who aren’t in school, allowing them to complete their work without needing instructions from the teacher. So, let us use our cell phones for this reason; please be kind and allow us to use phones in school! I would like to know what you said about us using phones. I think you would be an excellent principal, and that would help us in school. However, we will keep our cell phones turned off during classes.
