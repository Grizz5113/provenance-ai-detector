Technology capable of reading a student’s emotional expressions could be very valuable. This would make students’ lives in the classroom easier, offering so many advantages. It would recognize what a student is feeling and it could help to change that.

If a student was sad, then the computer would know and it could help make a difference. 

Some students might not want to do work or learn if they are sad, and this could change that and put a smile on their face. The author says, “if you smile when a Web ad appears on your screen, a similar ad might follow. But if you frown, the next ad will be different.” This could make the student have a better time in the classroom.

A student might be super confused and not know anything about what they are doing or they might just not understand the question; well, this technology could help that. It says, “A classroom computer could recognize when a student is becoming confused or bored, then it could modify the lesson, like an effective human instructor.” This means if the main teacher is telling someone else in a classroom how to do a problem but other people might also be struggling, then the computer could help them and see their emotion and it would change the question to try and make it more understandable.

This technology has the potential to help so many students and classrooms. With this, so much more could be learned in classrooms without kids getting bored. It could help students in the classroom get through so much more. Teachers wouldn’t have to be trying to hurry and help the people that don't understand out while the other people wait. This technology could be very valuable.
