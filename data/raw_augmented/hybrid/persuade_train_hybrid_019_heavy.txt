Considering a career with the Seagoing Cowboys? If you’re contemplating this unique opportunity, let me offer some compelling reasons and insight into what life is truly like within the program. Reflect on what excites you about spending time aboard their vessel – what possibilities do you envision?

I firmly believe that joining the Seagoing Cowboys would be an incredibly rewarding experience, primarily due to the chance to explore a remarkable array of locations. 

Life onboard offers a diverse range of activities and leisure pursuits. Often, after the animals were unloaded, the crew would engage in spirited games such as baseball and volleyball. Furthermore, certain roles require vigilance; some individuals are assigned to night watch, diligently monitoring the animal’s well-being. Throughout the day, feeding and watering the animals – a crucial task performed three times – ensures their continued health and utility. Failure to fulfill this responsibility would render the animals entirely useless.

My friend, Luke, served as a Seagoing Cowboy and exemplified these duties. He consistently fulfilled his night watch responsibilities, and after submitting his report one evening, he unfortunately fell down a staircase toward an opening leading to the ocean. Although he managed to catch himself, the fall resulted in broken ribs, temporarily preventing him from working. Remarkably, during this period of recovery, Luke discovered a newfound enjoyment for both onboard activities and exploring beyond the ship. The crew frequently played games within the animal pens when they were unloaded, including volleyball and baseball as previously mentioned.

The role of a Seagoing Cowboy demands considerable confidence and strength – you might be assigned to any task without prior knowledge. While aboard, there’s no retreat; you are positioned in the middle of the sea, making it impossible to return if a situation arises. 

Ultimately, joining the Seagoing Cowboys presents an exceptional opportunity for fun, friendship, and exploration. You could travel to new countries and witness genuinely unique environments. For some, experiencing a different culture can evoke a sense of nostalgia, like rediscovering something cherished from the past – perhaps visiting the homeland of your ancestors. This program would undoubtedly be an amazing endeavor. Of course, there will be challenges along the way, but with resilience, you can always overcome them. Imagining myself as part of this team, I would enthusiastically accept the opportunity because I relish pushing my personal boundaries and embracing new experiences.
