The electoral college is a component of the Constitution; it has been used to determine who will be our president for as long as the Constitution has existed on paper. Why get rid of it? Is it because we live in a world where everything is new and improved, or because it genuinely doesn’t work? Electoral colleges are widely regarded as an anachronistic and non-democratic method of selecting a president. So why do we need to change the way things are done?

The election would be thrown to the House of Representatives, where state delegations vote on the president; because each state casts only one vote, the single representative from Wyoming represents 500,000 voters. However, the electoral college avoids run-off elections, meaning no candidate receives a majority of the votes cast.
