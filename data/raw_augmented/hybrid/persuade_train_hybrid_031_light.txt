I believe students should be required to do community service. I think this because young people and adults are littering, and they should clean up for what they did, or even if they don’t, to help the government and the world save us and prevent us from being in this “economic conflict.” People should also do community service to help others, like participating in tutoring to assist those who need it. However, I believe they should complete their required coursework first; as long as they are helping someone, that’s good enough for me. For example, I tutor my brother and his friends who need help with homework or classwork. We all know not all students will want to participate in doing community service because they think they are too good for it, but we can at least try to convince people to help. Students might think community service is a punishment, but even if it is, they should be happy to help others. I also believe police officers should take more responsibility by doing some community service; I see them littering all the time. But kids my age should definitely be required to do community service. Students should help other students in anything like maybe homework or something. I get tutored in algebra by a friend of mine, and he takes time out of his day to help me. Some kids may not be able to do community service because they might have a health problem or need assistance themselves. However, students should be required to do community service if they don’t have any problems or something similar.
