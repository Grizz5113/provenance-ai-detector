Dear Principal,

I am writing to respectfully propose a reconsideration of the current policy regarding cell phone use during lunch and free periods. While I understand the desire to maintain focus and productivity within the classroom setting, I believe that restricting access to phones entirely during these times is unnecessarily restrictive and ultimately counterproductive. Students should be permitted to utilize their devices during lunch and free time, provided they are silenced or turned off during instructional hours.

The primary concern regarding cell phone use in class centers on distraction – a valid point. However, simply prohibiting access doesn’t address the root of the issue; it merely creates an environment where students feel restricted and potentially resentful. During these unstructured periods, phones can be valuable tools for communication with family, accessing information, or even engaging in quiet relaxation. To demand complete silence during these times feels unduly controlling.

Furthermore, I worry about the potential consequences of a strict policy regarding test preparation. If a student is reviewing material during free time and needs to quickly consult a resource on their phone, a rigid rule could inadvertently hinder their understanding and performance. 

I propose a system that balances responsible technology use with the school’s educational goals. Students should be required to silence or turn off their phones upon entering a classroom. To ensure compliance, teachers could implement a brief check-in at the beginning of class, asking students to demonstrate that their devices are powered down. For instances of repeated disregard for this rule – after receiving three warnings – disciplinary action, such as temporary removal of the phone from school grounds or parental notification, would be appropriate. 

Ultimately, I believe that trusting students to exercise responsible judgment regarding cell phone use during non-instructional times is a worthwhile endeavor. A more nuanced approach, focused on clear expectations and consistent consequences for misuse, would foster a greater sense of autonomy and respect among the student body. 

Sincerely,

Student
