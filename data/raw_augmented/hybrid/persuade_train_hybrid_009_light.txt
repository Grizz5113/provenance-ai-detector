Dear Principal,

I think students shouldn’t have at least a B average on their grades to participate in sports or other activities because some students try very hard in school but don’t achieve good grades. Also, many students enjoy sports or other activities but lack the necessary grades for participation.

Finally, it's simply not fair to students with bad grades who are also good and intelligent to be required to improve in order to participate in an activity.

There are students that try very hard in school but can’t get higher than a C on their grade, while others don’t even have to try to earn an A. 

But some students try even harder than those with an A, yet they can only achieve a C. It's not easy to obtain a good grade; it requires effort, and sometimes, despite our best efforts, things don’t work out as planned.

Many students love to participate in activities—it’s good to stay active and have fun in school. However, when some of that fun is taken away because of a specific grade requirement, it can become a problem for some students.
