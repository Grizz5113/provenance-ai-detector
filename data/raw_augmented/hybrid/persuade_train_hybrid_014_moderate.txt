Participating in the seagoing program was the best thing that ever happened to me. Even if it doesn’t seem particularly interesting, I highly recommend trying it. I know some people don’t like to get out much, but this experience is far superior to anything you can do indoors.

It is a very rewarding experience to go seagoing. You get to see a great many different places and meet diverse individuals with varied lifestyles. Sometimes it's fascinating hearing them talk, while other times it can be genuinely funny. The people also ask about our country; they’re curious about the people, food, and animals we have, as well as what our houses look like.

I believe you should go seagoing because it’s unlike anything else. Traveling the sea and witnessing the water as the sun sets is an unforgettable experience. I especially love the sound of the ocean; it calms me when I miss home. But sometimes, the ship feels just as comfortable as home itself.

I sincerely wish that you would go seagoing because it’s truly the best thing you will ever do. If it doesn't interest you, that’s perfectly alright – there are millions of other things to explore. Perhaps it simply isn’t for you, but for me, it feels completely right.
