I believe students should be required to complete community service hours. My reasoning stems from the increasing amount of littering I observe, and the potential for widespread action to address this issue, as well as mitigate what some perceive as an “economic conflict.” Furthermore, requiring community service would encourage individuals to actively help others, such as through tutoring programs. It’s important that students fulfill their academic obligations first, but assisting someone in need is a positive endeavor. For instance, I tutor my brother and his friends when they require assistance with homework or classwork. While many students may initially resist the idea of community service, viewing it perhaps as a punishment, even this participation could foster a sense of goodwill. I also believe law enforcement officers should be required to engage in community service, given that I frequently witness instances of littering by police personnel. Specifically, I think requiring community service for students would be beneficial; students can help other students with tasks like homework. I am fortunate enough to receive tutoring in algebra from a friend who willingly dedicates his time to assist me. However, it’s also important to acknowledge that some students may face health challenges or require personal assistance that prevents them from participating. Nevertheless, if a student does not have such obstacles, requiring community service would be a valuable experience.
