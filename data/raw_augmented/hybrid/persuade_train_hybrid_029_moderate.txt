If you’re tired of your job and looking for an adventure, you can come join the “Seagoing Cowboys.” You can help us bathe animals, send goods to those who need them most.

We have so much fun on the ship – we feed horses, bathe animals, and clean the stalls. But after that, we have plenty of time to play; you can play baseball, volleyball, table tennis, fencing, boxing, whittling, and the games help pass a lot of time since it takes such a long time to cross the ocean. The ship is just like a cruise, but you have to help with some chores. I had fun on the ship and I think you can too. Being a seagoing cowboy opened the world to me, and I was very grateful for this opportunity; it made me aware of people in other countries and their needs.

That’s what you can do or see being a seagoing cowboy – you can go to places like Greece. In Greece, which I thought was really special, was seeing the Acropolis. I also went to Venice, Italy, a city with streets of water. I hope you choose to become a seagoing cowboy and help out the people in need.
