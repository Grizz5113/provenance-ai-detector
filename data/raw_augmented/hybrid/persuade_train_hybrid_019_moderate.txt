Are you considering joining the Seagoing Cowboys? If so, I have plenty of reasons why you should and want to know what this program entails. Think about what it would be like to join them – what would *you* do as a Seagoing Cowboy?

I believe you should join the Seagoing Cowboys because it offers the chance to experience and visit many unique places.

When aboard the ship, there are numerous activities and games available. Sometimes, after the animals were unloaded, they would play baseball and volleyball. On occasion, crew members had to take night watch to ensure the animals remained safe and secure. During the day, everyone was responsible for feeding and watering the animals three times – if this duty wasn’t fulfilled, the animals would be useless.

Luke was a Seagoing Cowboy and he did exactly these things I described. Luke took his night watch responsibilities seriously, and after returning his report that evening, he unfortunately fell down stairs toward an opening to the ocean, but thankfully caught himself. This resulted in broken ribs, preventing him from working for a period. During this time, he also discovered opportunities for enjoyment on both the ship and ashore. They would play games in the animals’ pen when they were unloaded, like volleyball and baseball.

Being a Seagoing Cowboy requires confidence and strength; you could be selected for any job, though you wouldn't always know what it is. Once aboard the boat, there’s no going back because you are in the middle of the sea.

Joining the Seagoing Cowboys represents an opportunity to have fun, make friends, and explore. You can also visit new countries and discover unique places. Some people can truly take this chance to heart.

Sometimes traveling to a different country is like revisiting something you once loved – something you haven’t seen in a long time. That place you visit could be where your family originated from. Joining the Seagoing Cowboys would be an amazing experience. Of course, there will be challenges and setbacks, but you can always overcome them. Just imagine: if you join the Seagoing Cowboys, you can do many of the things you thought you couldn’t. That would be truly remarkable. If I had the opportunity to be a Seagoing Cowboy, I would absolutely say yes because I enjoy pushing my limits.
