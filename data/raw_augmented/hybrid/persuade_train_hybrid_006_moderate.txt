Dear Principal,

As a student at this school, I understand there have been concerns regarding cell phone use and you are considering changes to the current policy.

I have reviewed each of the proposed policies and believe the first option – allowing students to bring phones and use them during lunch and other free times – is the most effective approach. 

The reason I think this plan would work well is that sometimes students need to contact their parents for urgent matters, and restricting phone access wouldn’t detract from their education since it would occur during designated free time rather than in class.

I also believe that the second policy—prohibiting cell phones at school—could put a student in danger if they needed to call for assistance. 

I hope this letter provides you with insight into how students feel about these two proposed policies.
