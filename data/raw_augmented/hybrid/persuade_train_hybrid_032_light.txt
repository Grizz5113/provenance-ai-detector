Dear students,

I think it is right for elementary school children to pick up their litter. In this letter I want to express that if you help pick up the litter it makes you a better person in heart and refusing to do so is just terrible, I know if someone would ask me if I wanted to pick up litter for them I would say “yes, I would because it keeps this place clean.” But I don’t think students should be required to perform community service – yes, it would be a good thing to help the area where you live, but what if you have something to do or need to be somewhere or spend time with your family and friends? I personally think almost nobody would want to do community service; probably in a room full of 20-25 people I think only about maybe 5 or 6 of the people in that room would do it, but the other 15 or 14 people wouldn’t even try to see if they like it or if they feel good about it.

And as you know there have been many animal deaths because we don't pick up our trash or put it in a trash can – we just drop it on the floor or ground. But say that you were a deer and you were really hungry and there was nothing to eat, but when you spot a McDonald’s wrapper, the deer would eat it and eventually be really sick or it could possibly die. My sister, my mother, and I are in the Animal Protection Program “ASPCA” and we hate when people litter and we abolish it; we want everybody to take that into consideration sincerely.
