Pavlovian response -- good for some , bad for others . I was young and impatient , so the sound was like a very slow starting engine when all I wanted was to be moving . For me it dials up memories of having to wait , wait , wait to get to the Internet . Add to that the fact that dial up really was painfully slow at times . Broadband was a godsend . Thereafter , the dial - up sound triggered feelings of anticipation and frustration , making it unbearable . But now it just reminds me of childhood , so I sort of like it .
