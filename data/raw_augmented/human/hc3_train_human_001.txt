Figurehead ? No sir he leads the entire Catholic church in a very real sense . The pope is considered the one man on Earth closest to God , he has the power to set new doctrine , change church stances , call councils and much more . To give you an idea of the power invested in the papacy there is a belief called infallibility , when the Pope speaks inf an official capacity on church dogma it is literally impossible for him to be incorrect . And yes there was once a time where the Pope had more tangible power , actually acting as more of a king then spiritual leader , but the office remains one of the oldest of its kind in all of human history and the pope does remain very influential on many levels . His Holiness remains arguably one of the most powerful men on earth , leading a flock of over one billion faithful .
