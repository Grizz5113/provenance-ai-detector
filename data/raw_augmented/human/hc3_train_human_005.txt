Those stories are just bad science reporting . * Nobody , * repeat * nobody , * takes seriously the sci - fi notion that we 're " living in a computer simulation . " That 's just silly . What you 're * probably * thinking of is a series of experiments that were designed to put lower bounds on the discrete scale of space . The question remains open whether space is continuous , or whether it has a smallest meaningful scale . Like as if I told you that nothing can be smaller than an inch across , or that anything which looks smaller than an inch ca n't be localized to within less than an inch . That 's obviously not true , but if you replace " inch " with a very small distance , you 'll get the gist of the thing . Trouble is , every experiment so far conducted says that the * maximum * lower bound on distance is far , far smaller than the smallest thing anybody knows how to measure . So the smart money is still on space being continuous , not discrete .
