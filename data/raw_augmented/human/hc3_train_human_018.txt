" The N'th " as in " The second " is usually used only in royalty . It 's used when somebody has the same name as an ancestor . Note ; not necessarily the father / mother , so it can skip generations . If some King George would have grand - grand children and one of those would be named George again and he would be King in his time and there would be no George being King in between the two then the second George would be called George the second . Junior is usually only used within two consecutive generations . If you name your child the same as your name or your spouses name then that child will essentially be Junior while you 're Senior .
