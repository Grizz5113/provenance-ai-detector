It is generally not advisable to refinance a mortgage just before selling a property. This is because the process of refinancing a mortgage can be lengthy and costly, and the benefits of refinancing may not outweigh the costs if you are planning to sell the property in the near future.There are a few situations where refinancing before selling might make sense, however. For example, if you are able to secure a significantly lower interest rate through refinancing, it could potentially save you a significant amount of money on your monthly mortgage payments. If you are planning to sell the property within a few years, this could be a worthwhile consideration.It is also possible that you may be able to negotiate a higher selling price for the property if you have recently refinanced and have a lower mortgage balance. In this case, the potential increase in the selling price could offset the costs of refinancing.Overall, it is important to carefully consider the costs and benefits of refinancing before making a decision. You may want to speak with a financial advisor or mortgage lender to help you determine if refinancing before selling is a good choice for your situation.
