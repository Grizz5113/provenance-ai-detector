It is important to first discuss your symptoms with your healthcare provider, as they will be able to determine the cause of your lower back pain and recommend the most appropriate treatment. Some possible causes of lower back pain after Implanon insertion could include muscle strain or spasm, irritation of a nerve, or a reaction to the implant itself.

If your lower back pain is severe or persistent, your healthcare provider may recommend over-the-counter or prescription pain medication to help manage your symptoms. They may also suggest physical therapy or other types of rehabilitation to help strengthen the muscles in your back and improve your overall mobility. In some cases, your healthcare provider may recommend that you have the Implanon removed if it is causing significant discomfort or other side effects.

It is important to follow your healthcare provider's recommendations for managing your lower back pain and to seek medical attention if your symptoms worsen or if you experience any other unusual symptoms.
