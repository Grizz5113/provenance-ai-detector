When you buy a home, you may be required to pay a brokerage fee to the real estate agent or broker who represents you in the transaction. The brokerage fee is a percentage of the purchase price of the home and is typically paid by the buyer at the time of closing. The amount of the brokerage fee can vary depending on the market, the complexity of the transaction, and the services provided by the agent or broker.In some cases, the seller may agree to pay the brokerage fee as part of the terms of the sale. This is known as a "seller's concession" and is typically negotiated as part of the overall terms of the sale.It is important to carefully review the terms of your agreement with the real estate agent or broker to understand any fees that may be charged and when they are due. It is also a good idea to shop around and compare fees from different agents or brokers to ensure that you are getting the best deal.
