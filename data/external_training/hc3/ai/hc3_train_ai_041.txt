There are a few things to consider when trading a large number of shares:Spread: The spread is the difference between the bid price (the highest price someone is willing to pay for a stock) and the ask price (the lowest price someone is willing to sell a stock). When trading a large number of shares, it is important to pay attention to the spread to ensure that you are getting a fair price.Slippage: Slippage is the difference between the price you expect to pay for a stock and the actual price you end up paying. When trading a large number of shares, you may experience slippage as the market moves against you. To minimize slippage, you can use limit orders instead of market orders.Market impact: Trading a large number of shares can also impact the market price of the stock. This is known as market impact. To minimize market impact, you can use techniques such as "iceberg" orders, which only show a portion of the order to the market at a time.Commission: Finally, you will also want to consider the commission you will pay to your broker for executing the trade. Some brokers charge a flat fee per trade, while others charge a percentage of the trade value. It is important to compare the fees charged by different brokers before making a decision.
