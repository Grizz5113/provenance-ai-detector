Do you know what a mosaic is ? It 's a picture made of tiny coloured stones , or shards of glass . Each piece has only one colour , and its shape has nothing to do with the picture itself . The image on your screen is essentially a mosaic . The tiny pieces are the pixels , millions of them . Each pixel only has a single colour , and its shape is a boring rectangle . By making these pixels glow in the right pattern , an image is displayed . Note that stuff like text on the screen is also an image . The reason why you can not see the tiny pieces , but instead seem to see a smooth image , is because there are so many pixels .
