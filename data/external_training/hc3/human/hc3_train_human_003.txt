Counting cards does n't try to guess what the next card will be . When playing blackjack , the player has an advantage if there are a high proportion of face cards left in the deck . Face cards help give the player Blackjacks and help the dealer bust , and they give the player better odds with side bets like insurance , splits , and double downs . So basically , card counting is a betting strategy . If you keep track of how many face cards you see being dealt versus how many non - face cards , then you 'll know what proportion of face cards there are left in the deck . If there 's a high percentage of face cards left in the deck , the player should bet more money because they have a slight advantage . And that 's all card counting does : helps you bet the right amount of money in order to boost your chances by a percentage point or so . There are a few different techniques for card counting , but basically you assign points to different type of cards and also keep track of how many we 're dealt ( so that you can figure out proportions ) .
