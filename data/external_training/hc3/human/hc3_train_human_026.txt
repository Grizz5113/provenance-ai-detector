So I think most of this has to do with aerodynamics . The front of the car is designed to " slice " through the air instead of pushing it . The back is * usually * an after thought aerodynamically . Now consider hatchbacks , like my Crosstrek ( Subaru ) . It has a rear windshield wiper like most hatchbacks . Why ? When driving , the air passing over the car ends up getting sucked into the back window and panels . This is because the shape of the car forms more of a vacuum there . You can notice this effect easily during a light rain . You may not need to turn on your windshield wipers but you soon notice your rear view window misted over . You can see this in tractor trailers as well .
