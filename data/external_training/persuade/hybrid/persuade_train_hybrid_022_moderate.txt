I am a Seagoing Cowboy. I have experienced many different places – Greece, Europe, China, and more. You should definitely become a Seagoing Cowboy because you get to experience places you wouldn’t otherwise encounter.

When you are a Seagoing Cowboy, you make many friends. You get to help take care of horses, cows, and mules. By 1947, I made nine different trips, the most of any seagoing Cowboy! Not only do you have fun doing all this, but you also get prepared for hard work in the future. We also played fun games such as volleyball and table tennis; we fenced, boxed, read, and whittled to pass the time. Being a Seagoing Cowboy makes you much more aware about the world and its people. There are many things countries need, and it’s a truly enriching experience. You also have lots of fun on board, especially on return trips from unloading the animals. All of the fun on board made the month-long trip to China seem short. When I was in Greece, I even got to see the Acropolis! It was very special. I also turned 18 before arriving in Greece, so I could be drafted into the military. We finally arrived on August 14th, which was the day the Pacific War ended!

In conclusion, I would definitely recommend becoming a Seagoing Cowboy; it is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity that is both very fun and educational.
