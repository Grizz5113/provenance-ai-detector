When people ask for advice, they are because they’re in a bad situation and really need it the most. They mainly ask people they trust and know—because they know the most about them and the best ways to help them. They often go to family members and close friends. Sometimes, if they are in school, they might seek guidance from a trusted adult, such as a guidance counselor, a teacher, principal, or police officer if one is available at the school.

The reason people ask for advice is because they don’t know how to do things right and they may not be feeling well. It could be something going on at home they need help with—like their mom and dad are struggling, somebody in their family passed away, or they're simply going through rough times. They don’t know how to deal with it; they just need some help and advice on how to handle the situation. It isn’t bad to ask for help and advice.

It is a good thing to ask multiple people because there are different kinds of situations. You never know what the situation is or how serious it is. Asking multiple people allows you to figure out which one is best and see if that fixes the problem. If that doesn't work, you have the other people’s ideas to help solve it. And they’re always there for you to help you out with anything if you need them most. Plus, it never hurts to ask for advice mainly when you need it most.
