My position on driverless cars is bad because I don’t trust them. I have a couple of reasons why I don’t trust them. My first reason is accidents. Driverless cars could have a system malfunction and drive off course, something breaks, and they crash into something. This could happen to anyone such as police, buses, or simple pedestrians.

My second reason is too many driverless cars. If there were to make way too many driverless cars then it would create a lot of traffic because there are a lot of cars who need a driver to drive. Such as if they were to go to the junkyard the junkyards would overflow and they would have to make more and more, and there would be less places to build houses or less crops for farmers.

My last reason is pollution. If a lot of people bought all these driverless cars then so much pollution would come up and take over the sky and people would get sick and die from breathing in the air. If they were to change gas into something else and something went wrong with the gas or something to run the cars, they would either blow up or cause too much pollution that they would have to refund all the people and people would stop buying these because they wouldn’t trust them.

That’s why I say no to driverless cars.
