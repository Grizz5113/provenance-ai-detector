Dear Principal,

I am writing to express my concerns regarding the proposed policy that would restrict participation in sports or extracurricular activities for any student with a below-B average. I believe this policy is fundamentally unfair to many of our athletic students and coaches. Typically, sports teams include students who maintain C averages, and it’s often a challenging balance to manage academic work alongside competitive athletics. If implemented as proposed, we risk significantly reducing team sizes because players are already stressed trying to perform at a high level while maintaining a C average. It would undoubtedly be an excessive burden to simultaneously dedicate the necessary time and effort to both academics and sports. While a B average would provide a more manageable target, it could potentially lead some students to prioritize one area over another, risking a decline in their overall grades.
