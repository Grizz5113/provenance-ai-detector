Dear Principal,

I believe students should be permitted to use cell phones during certain times because I think it could benefit our learning environment. Another reason to allow phone usage is during lunch; this would enable students to communicate with friends without having to wait until after school. During lunchtime, we can text our friends and, if needed, quickly contact our parents if we forget homework. This immediate access would prevent us from receiving a zero on assignments.

Furthermore, cell phones could be used to photograph our homework assignments, allowing us to share them with classmates who may be absent. Students could complete their work independently without needing constant reminders from the teacher. I urge you to consider allowing this use of technology to support student learning—please let us utilize our phones in school! I appreciate your consideration and believe that this would ultimately benefit us as students. We will, of course, keep our cell phones turned off during classes.
