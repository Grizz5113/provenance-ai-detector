Dear Principal,

As a student at this school, I understand there have been problems involving cell phones and you are interested in changing the policy on cell phone use during school hours.

I have looked at each policy and I believe it is a better option to go with the first policy – allowing students to bring phones and use them during lunch and other free times.

The reason that I think this plan will work better is because sometimes students need to call their parents to tell them things, and that would not take away from the education they receive since it would be on the student’s own free time and not in class.

I also believe that the second policy – prohibiting cell phones at school – could put a student in danger if he or she needed to call for help.

I hope this letter helped you by giving you a look at how the students feel about the two new policies you are considering changing.
