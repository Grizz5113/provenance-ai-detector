Dear Students,

I believe it is important for elementary school children to pick up their litter. In this letter, I want to express my belief that helping to clean up litter makes us better people, and refusing to do so would be regrettable. If someone asked me if I wanted to help them pick up litter, I would say “yes,” because it keeps our surroundings clean. However, I don’t think students should be required to perform community service; while assisting in our local area would be beneficial, what about commitments we already have – activities, family obligations, or time with friends? Personally, I believe that very few people would willingly participate in community service. In a room of 20-25 individuals, perhaps only five or six would actively engage, while the remaining fifteen or fourteen might not even attempt to explore whether they enjoy it or feel good about contributing.

As you know, numerous animal deaths occur because we don’t properly dispose of our trash – we simply drop it on the ground. For example, if a deer were hungry and spotted a discarded McDonald's wrapper, it would likely eat it and subsequently become seriously ill or even die. My sister, my mother, and I are involved in the ASPCA Animal Protection Program, and we strongly oppose littering. We want everyone to consider this issue carefully. Sincerely, STUDENT_NAME
