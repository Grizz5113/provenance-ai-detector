If you find yourself yearning for an adventure after a monotonous job, I urge you to consider joining the “Seagoing Cowboys.” Our mission involves providing vital assistance – from bathing animals to delivering supplies to those most in need.

Life aboard our ship is surprisingly rewarding. We spend our days caring for horses and other animals, diligently cleaning the stalls, and ensuring everything runs smoothly. However, after completing these essential tasks, there’s ample time for recreation. The crew enjoys a wide range of activities, including baseball, volleyball, table tennis, fencing, boxing, and even whittling – all of which help to fill the long hours during our ocean crossings.

The ship itself is akin to a cruise experience, but with an added responsibility to contribute to the ship’s operations. I personally found my time as a “Seagoing Cowboy” incredibly fulfilling, and I genuinely believe anyone considering this opportunity would too. It truly opened my eyes to the world and fostered a deep sense of gratitude for this unique experience. Most significantly, it broadened my understanding of people in different countries and their diverse needs.

Beyond the ship’s activities, there are incredible opportunities for exploration. For example, I had the chance to visit Greece, where the iconic Acropolis left me utterly captivated. Furthermore, a trip to Venice, Italy – a city built on canals – was an unforgettable experience. I sincerely hope you will consider joining the “Seagoing Cowboys” and contributing your efforts to assisting those in need.
