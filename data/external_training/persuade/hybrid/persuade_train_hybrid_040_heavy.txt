Dear Principal,

I am writing to express my serious concerns regarding the proposed policy stipulating that any student with a grade below a B average will be prohibited from participating in sports or extracurricular activities. I believe this regulation is fundamentally unfair to many of our athletic students and coaches. It’s frequently observed that sports teams consist of individuals maintaining C averages, often struggling to perform at a competitive level. Implementing this policy would inevitably lead to significantly reduced team sizes, as the added pressure to maintain a C average while simultaneously competing in a sport would create an untenable burden for many student-athletes. 

The reality is that students involved in sports are already facing considerable demands on their time and energy. Requiring them to consistently achieve a B average – or higher – while also dedicating themselves to rigorous athletic training would likely exacerbate academic difficulties, potentially leading to a decline in performance across all subjects. While the intention behind this policy may be to encourage academic improvement, it risks creating an environment where students become overly focused on one area at the expense of others, ultimately jeopardizing their overall success.
