Everyday people die in car accidents due to the use of cell phones while driving. According to research, cell phones are leading factors contributing to these accidents. Studies show that a cell phone puts drivers at four times greater risk of a collision. Despite this danger, people continue to use cell phones because they want to remain available and connected with others, often believing that hand-free sets are safe. Furthermore, some argue that cell phones are necessary in emergency situations. Although some support using cell phones while driving, there is evidence demonstrating their dangerousness and the need for individuals to avoid talking while operating a vehicle.

Cell phone users, such as lawyers or businesspeople, frequently claim they must always be available to answer calls to maintain their businesses; this can create dangerous and hazardous situations like collisions or accidents. Business-related stress and distractions while driving can trigger emotional reactions that lead to tragic outcomes. Because of the conversation, drivers may become upset, impairing their ability to drive safely and potentially leading to a loss of control. Therefore, if people do not make the conscious decision to put away their cell phones, their habits can harm innocent individuals.

Opponents argue that hands-free sets are safer because they allow drivers to use both hands. However, hands-free devices do not eliminate the risk of causing an accident and may even worsen it by suggesting a false sense of security. Research shows that using headsets can be as dangerous as holding a phone because conversations distract drivers from focusing on the road. Scientists have proven that when people talk on the phone, they tend to focus on the person they are speaking to, which does not interfere with driving. The problem arises when a car swerves unexpectedly or a pedestrian steps into traffic; in these situations, the mind lacks the processing power to react quickly enough. It’s not simply holding the wheel and cell phone simultaneously that may cause an accident due to lack of maneuverability, but rather the distraction caused by conversation and the resulting delayed reaction at a critical time.

Opponents also contend that cell phones are beneficial because they provide an opportunity to make a call in case of emergency. 

I believe that people should be more responsible with how they use their phones; simply put, individuals need to prioritize safety over connectivity.
