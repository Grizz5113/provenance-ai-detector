Limiting car usage has advantages. For example, stress can happen when you have a car, but if you don’t have one, you tend to be happier, as Heidrun Walter stated in her story. People who don't drive often use smart planning and are more comfortable in suburban life, where not having a car is more compact and accessible to public transportation. This means people would save money on gas and other expenses instead of spending it on car insurance. Furthermore, suburban areas now have stores within walking distance.

Also, limiting car usage means less pollution. Without car emissions trapped from cold nights and warm days, there would be no smog. Cars are stressful, and rather than using a car, one could use a two-seated bike. Cities can make many new things instead of relying on cars, which are so much distractions.
