It is true that cell phones are distracting during class time, yet they have become increasingly necessary in today’s society. This technology has evolved far beyond a simple telephone and now represents an integral part of our daily lives. Cell phones have transformed into handheld computers that we carry with us everywhere, including school – a development that could represent the next step toward a more advanced educational future.

I am not claiming that students should be allowed cell phones during class periods; however, I recognize the potential difficulties associated with such a change. While students should be permitted to utilize any technology offered during their free time, I find it confusing when there are so many restrictions – otherwise, what is the purpose of calling it “free time”? With their cell phones, students can also email and work on certain papers or projects, which undoubtedly aids them in their studies.

Students should still have their phones turned off during class periods because they undeniably cause a distraction, regardless of how helpful they may be. Even if this is a current challenge with existing rules prohibiting cell phone use, allowing students to utilize cell phones out of class might reduce the temptation to use them within the classroom setting.

Therefore, I believe it is in the schools’ best interest to allow cell phones during student free time.
