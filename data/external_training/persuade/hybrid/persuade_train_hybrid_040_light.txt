Dear Principal,

I am addressing the new policy you are considering changing. The new policy—that any students with below a B average cannot participate in sports or other activities—seems unfair to many athletic students and coaches. Usually, sports teams have students with C averages; they barely can play. If this policy is applied, there would not be many players on a team. The players are already stressed enough to play a sport and maintain a C average. It would be too much of a struggle to keep up with all their work and participate in a sport. At least at a B average, it would be manageable; the idea could encourage some students to focus on a class to improve their grade, but they might inadvertently neglect other subjects and fall behind.
