Dear Principal,

I believe that students should not be required to maintain at least a B average in their coursework to participate in sports or other extracurricular activities. This is because some students work incredibly hard academically but struggle with achieving high grades, while many others are passionate about participating in activities but do not meet the minimum grade requirements. 

It seems inherently unfair to students who have lower grades but demonstrate intelligence and dedication, requiring them to improve their academic standing simply to participate. There are students who consistently put forth significant effort in school yet can only attain a C average, while others may achieve A’s with less exertion. 

However, some students work even harder than those with As, yet they still manage only a C. Achieving good grades requires considerable effort, and sometimes despite our best efforts, outcomes don't align with our intentions. 

Participation in activities is valuable; it promotes physical activity and enjoyment within the school environment. However, when these opportunities are restricted by specific grade requirements, it can be a frustrating experience for some students.
